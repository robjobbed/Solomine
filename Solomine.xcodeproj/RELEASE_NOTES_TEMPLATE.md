# Release Notes Template

Use this template when creating releases on GitHub.

## Creating a Release on GitHub

1. Go to your repository on GitHub
2. Click "Releases" (right sidebar)
3. Click "Create a new release"
4. Fill in the details using the template below
5. Click "Publish release"

## v1.0.0 - Initial Release

**Release Date:** February 14, 2026

### 🎉 Welcome to Solomine!

Solomine is a modern iOS marketplace that connects talented builders with clients looking to bring their ideas to life. This is our initial release!

### ✨ Features

#### Authentication & Onboarding
- 🔐 **X (Twitter) OAuth Integration** - Secure sign-in with your X account
- 👤 **Role Selection** - Choose to join as a Builder or Client
- 🎨 **Beautiful Onboarding** - Smooth, intuitive first-time experience

#### Profile Management
- 💼 **Rich Profiles** - Showcase your skills, experience, and portfolio
- 📝 **Customizable Bio** - Tell your story
- 🏷️ **Skills & Tags** - Highlight what you're great at
- 💰 **Rate Setting** - Set your hourly or project rates

#### Discovery
- 🔍 **Browse Builders** - Find talented developers for your project
- 📱 **Browse Projects** - Discover opportunities as a builder
- 🎯 **Smart Filtering** - Find exactly what you're looking for
- 💫 **Beautiful Cards** - Clean, modern interface

#### Communication
- 💬 **Direct Messaging** - Built-in chat system
- 📨 **Real-time Updates** - Stay in sync with your conversations
- 🔔 **Message Notifications** - Never miss an important message

#### Customization
- 🎨 **Theme System** - Choose from three beautiful themes:
  - Matte Black - Classic, professional dark theme
  - Midnight Blue - Rich blue tones with depth
  - Forest Green - Earthy, natural aesthetic
- ⚙️ **Comprehensive Settings** - Customize your experience

#### Legal & Support
- 📄 **Terms of Service** - Full legal documentation
- 🔒 **Privacy Policy** - CCPA & GDPR compliant
- 📧 **Contact Support** - Easy access to help (rob@solomine.io)

### 🛠 Technical Highlights

- **Built with SwiftUI** - Modern, declarative UI
- **iOS 16.0+** - Supports latest iOS features
- **MVVM Architecture** - Clean, maintainable codebase
- **Swift Concurrency** - Async/await throughout
- **Dark Mode First** - Beautiful in any lighting condition
- **Performance Optimized** - Smooth 60fps animations

### 📱 Requirements

- iOS 16.0 or later
- iPhone or iPad
- Internet connection for authentication and sync
